PROJECT FOUNDATIONS

The original community governance model comes from the YADEI Community Board System / CivicOS proposal. The platform should enable structured citizen participation, grassroots accountability, community service monitoring and lawful institutional cooperation.

The platform must not falsely claim statutory government authority. Community boards are participatory, advisory and monitoring bodies unless applicable law or signed institutional agreements grant additional powers.

NATIONAL ORGANIZATION

Support the following hierarchy:

1. National Secretariat
2. Six geopolitical coordinating zones
3. State/FCT coordination
4. 774 Local Government Areas / Area Councils
5. Wards
6. Communities and settlements
7. Optional polling-unit geographic references and community meeting points

State/FCT coordination is an operational software layer. It must not silently replace the governance hierarchy adopted by the organization.

Use verified, versioned geography datasets. Do not fabricate ward, polling-unit, settlement or government identifiers.

SEVEN MANDATORY THEMATIC BOARDS

EDU — Board of Education

SEC — Board of Security and Intelligence Gathering

TRD — Board of Trade, Investment and Tourism
YTH — Board of Skills and Youth Development
CUL — Board of Culture and Moral Values

All seven board definitions must be included in the initial database seed.

Allow each authorized jurisdiction to establish instances of these boards through a controlled approval workflow.
